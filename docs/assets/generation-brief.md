# Dyntree M1E artwork handoff

Built-in imagegen produced 36 renders, of which 35 are ready for use. All images are original raster automotive concept renders. Each asset used one generation call, and subsequent renders used the master or a verified matching view as references. Exact generation prompts and per-file color, trim, wheel, interior, angle, category, dimensions and byte size are recorded in manifest.json.

The visual foundation is a Graphite compact premium electric two-door 2+2 coupe, with a long wheelbase, short overhangs, flowing roof, muscular rear haunches, black panoramic roof, flush handles, closed nose with a lower cooling slit, full-width white front and red rear light bars with Y branches, tiny Y emblems, a discreet active spoiler and a diffuser without exhausts.

The master is m1e-master-graphite-front-3q.png. It also serves as the Graphite paint option and Divide front three-quarter view, avoiding duplicate files. All six other paint views share its exact camera and body. Trim front/rear comparisons retain Graphite paint. Divide uses 19 Aero wheels; Subtract uses 19 Dynamic wheels; Add and Multiply use 20 Performance wheels, with darker and carbon accents on Multiply.

The cabin has separate landscape center and driver screens, a conventional Y-detail steering wheel, physical climate and hazard controls, a console with wireless charging tray and two cupholders, and four seats. Graphite, Cloud, Copper and Multiply Sport cockpit options use the same dashboard and camera. Separate photos document the dashboard, front seats, two rear seats and console.

Use m1e-angle-side-v2.png as the side profile. The earlier m1e-angle-side.png had an unwanted rear-quarter seam and is marked superseded in the manifest. Retain it only as source history; exclude it from the final gallery. Other ready gallery views cover front, rear, elevated front, headlight and taillight details, the rear-left charging port, and night, road and charging-station scenes.

No requested image categories remain ungenerated in this scoped 35-asset set. Standalone interface mockups and sensor diagrams are intentionally assigned to the owner for code-native implementation. Full combinatorial paint × trim × wheel renders were not part of this scoped set; the seven paint comparisons, four trim pairs, three wheel closeups and four cockpit themes are represented separately.
